(Figma Community file "VLC Redesign (Community)"), compiled into VLCIcons.ttf.

  Design:  Juxtopposed - https://www.juxtopposed.com
  Figma:   https://www.figma.com/design/5UEUReDQz2dQjwoQDHY5z9/VLC-Redesign--Community-
  Video:   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ERFeOgqk324

https://github.com/SpeedyPete25/Juxtopposed-VLC-Rebuild (design/figma-assets).
